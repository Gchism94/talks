Two kinds of knowing

Experience meets explanation.

Mētis

Practical, situated know-how

Judgment built through doing: timing, habits, and workarounds.

Illustrative field note
“Before I close the form,
I copy my answer.”

Epistēmē

Systematic, reasoned knowledge

Principles we can explain, examine, and apply beyond one situation.

A principle to examineMake system status visible.

Can someone tell that their work saved?

Experience informs our ideas. Explicit explanations let us question them together.

Our brief for the AI

Give it the reasons
behind the design.

Person + situation · Who is using this, and what are they trying to do?

Evidence + choice · What happened? Which idea did we choose, and why?

Limits · Keep what works. Build one flow with sample data.

Test · What should someone be able to do with the result?

Ask the AI to state its assumptions before it starts building.
