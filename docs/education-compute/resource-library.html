Three models, three scopes

The talk’s 480-seat calculator illustrates college-scale demand. The route assessment models 80 seats on Jetstream2 with VM-specific SUs. This CloudBank request describes an 80-student CPU teaching pilot plus up to six staff identities; its initial student/shared data request is 440 GB before staff/overhead, with accounting and any expansion confirmed by CloudBank. These are different planning scenarios, not interchangeable provider budgets or awarded capacities.

Reading the evidence correctly

Current quota, loss of a benefit, new-account restriction, complete shutdown and a temporary access incident are different claims. Federal development funding, current operating support, state subsidy and an approved no-cost account are also different facts. Working storage and course hosting are separate from preservation.

Draft budgets, proposed tests and platform documentation do not establish actual classroom access, account limits or educational outcomes. Raw captures and the complete portable review archive stay in the local review folder; this public site contains reviewed outputs.

Proposal, not a service announcement. No CloudBank request, allocation, staffing appointment or pilot budget is approved by this site. Provider policies and proposed tests are separate from completed classroom evidence.
