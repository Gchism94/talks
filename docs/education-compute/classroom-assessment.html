From federal resources to a classroom service

UArizona Information Science · October 12, 2026 · Documentation assessment; illustrative workload model

Recommendation

Build a reliable CPU classroom first.
Add specialized resources one component at a time.

Use ACCESS for the large CPU and GPU allocation and the classroom pilot. Use campus UITS/AWS only to test CPU components, and free GPU resources to benchmark workloads before piloting. Size the ACCESS request from measured demand; consider CloudBank through ACCESS only if its managed service can meet the confirmed requirements.

Three findings change the plan. CloudBank has a general ACCESS teaching route, but its fall 2026 GPU pilot is full. MESA uses the learner’s existing CyVerse quota. PATh’s published affiliated-program list does not explicitly include IDSS; MESA funding is not automatic access.

Two representative course workloads

Data science and library/information science are the planning pair, not approved courses or named instructor commitments. Start with one assignment per course before a full-term service.

Data science · 40 seats

Python/R data analysis, reproducible notebooks, small-model CPU labs; optional grouped GPU experiment.

Fallback: Small CPU datasets and downloadable environment/materials.

Library and information science · 40 seats

Metadata normalization, collection analysis, public-corpus text processing, reproducible workflow and repository export.

Fallback: Local small-record workflow and institution-supported storage; no private patron or grade records on batch resources.

The first canary covers DS and MLIS. Notebook-compatible MS/BS Information Science and PhD projects can reuse the route after workload review. ETS and Information Science and Arts need public-data/browser workflows and durable exhibit hosting. Game Design/Development require a separate engine, graphics, licensing and desktop-access assessment; this notebook pilot does not establish Unreal/Unity or Windows support. Move from a component canary to two courses

Proposed timing and acceptance targets. Every gate is currently untested or unconfirmed; document success before promoting a service to required coursework.

  1. Scope and owners

    Before any class dependency · Course lead + college service owner

    Choose real course/syllabus, data classification, operator and backup; fund support and durable storage for the whole term.

    Stop if: No named owner or approved service period.

    Status: Not yet tested/confirmed

  2. Provider and access

    6–8 weeks before term (proposed) · Service owner + provider liaison

    Confirm approved route, course eligibility, actual roster/login, quotas, images, costs, GPU status and data/expiry terms.

    Stop if: Unresolved 80-seat provisioning or missing account/award.

    Status: Not yet tested/confirmed

  3. Component canary

    4–6 weeks before term (proposed) · One instructor + up to ten consenting volunteers

    Run one representative DS assignment and one public-records MLIS workflow; use a fixed image and retain launch, save/restart/export evidence.

    Stop if: Core software or save/export path fails.

    Status: Not yet tested/confirmed

  4. Capacity rehearsal

    2–4 weeks before term (proposed) · Operator + teaching assistants

    Test approved peak concurrency with provider permission; measure queue/spawn latency, CPU/RAM, storage and actual billed idle time. Suggested p95 login-to-workspace target: five minutes, to negotiate with provider.

    Stop if: Unmeasured or insufficient class capacity; credit/storage forecast exceeds award.

    Status: Not yet tested/confirmed

  5. Recovery and teach-out

    Before course admission · Primary and backup operators + Libraries liaison

    Restore files and service onto a second host; verify cross-student isolation, learner export and grade reconciliation. Proposed RPO 24 hours/RTO one business day require measurement.

    Stop if: No independent restorable backup or viable offline/CPU fallback.

    Status: Not yet tested/confirmed

  6. Two-course pilot

    One approved 15-week term · Course leads + operator

    80 illustrative seats; required work CPU-capable. GPU/OSPool/MESA modules only after their own gate passes; collect support time, billing and instructional outcomes.

    Stop if: Unsafe access, recurring capacity failure or no funded support.

    Status: Not yet tested/confirmed

  7. Retire or renew

    Decision at least 30 days before expiry (proposed) · Course leads + service owner + Libraries

    Export students/grades, reconcile submissions, deposit approved public outputs with Libraries; confirm next-term project/renewal route and verify deletion.

    Stop if: Assuming automatic education extension or treating grace periods as service funding.

    Status: Not yet tested/confirmed

Federal allocations supply bounded resources. College recurring operating funds cover course support, image maintenance, identity/rosters, backups, durable app/database services, export and Libraries stewardship. Cost these duties with actual operator-hours, provider terms and institutional rates before approval; no unsupported dollar estimate or staffing commitment is supplied.

Questions to resolve before a request

Draft questions for review; none has been sent to a provider or program officer.

CloudBank Classroom
  • Can the general ACCESS Classroom route support two non-AI/AI-mixed courses and 80 seats with 2 vCPU/6 GB/20 GB per seat? What are the actual limits and credit/budget terms?
  • Which packages, version pinning, notebook distribution, grading/export and account-isolation features are supported?
  • What are roster provisioning, CILogon/MFA, support hours, independent export/backup and shutdown dates?
  • How does the one-year renewal text align with ACCESS education-term/no-typical-extension policy? Is next term a renewal or a new request?
  • Please confirm current GPU pilot status and the 2027 request window; required activities will remain CPU-capable.
Jetstream2
  • Confirm CPU/GPU/storage exchanges, 164-vCPU dedicated or 196-vCPU shared peak quota, applicable flavor availability and required public/private network quotas.
  • Can 2 TB working data plus additional root/image/log/snapshot space be approved? Which shared home/filesystem architecture fits the workload and policy?
  • Review a hub or VM autoscaling/shutdown design and independent offsite backup/restore plan; verify classroom identity and grading service rules.
  • What renewal/new-course request, warning and deletion process applies to the exact awarded project?
NRP
  • For hosted versus private hubs, what class quota, GPU configuration, roster approval, software/image and support arrangements apply?
  • Can 80 homes have 20 GB each? What session, exception, persistence and end-of-course export rules apply to this particular hub?
  • Which model/API quotas and data-handling terms apply? What GPU fallback is acceptable when shared capacity is busy?
MESA / CyVerse
  • Can every eligible UArizona student receive the documented Pro/TRIF benefit, and what class/workshop enrollment limits and maintenance windows apply?
  • How are per-user CPU units, concurrency and storage charged across the selected portal app? Is GPU permission available for this specific assignment?
  • Demonstrate saving under data-store, Save & Exit, restarting, export and independent restoration for the chosen metadata app.
OSPool
  • Does the proposed public-data workflow fit the guest four-hour session? Confirm full roster, account dates, fair-share behavior, job/I/O sizes and training-policy requirements for semester use.
  • Which packaging, checkpointing, licensed-software or data-transfer restrictions apply? Agree on outputs retained elsewhere and a CPU/local fallback.
PATh / NSF program officer
  • Is IDSS award 2632685/MESA eligible under the affiliated-program policy, and can this particular research/teaching workload be covered?
  • If eligible, what startup prototype and job specification are needed for a full credit request, and which program officer should receive it?
College / Libraries
  • Who is the primary operator, backup and course escalation contact? What recurring support, storage, identity and hosting/database budget is approved?
  • Which student/grade/research data may go to each provider, for how long, and what accessibility/procurement review is required?
  • Who owns independent backups, second-host recovery, export/grade reconciliation and archival selection? What measured workload and educational outcomes justify scaling?
