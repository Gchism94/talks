The proposal in three commitments

Keep required work on CPU

Reproducible DS notebooks and MLIS metadata/database exercises. GPUs, MESA and throughput pools are optional modules with their own gates.

Pay for continuity

Named primary and backup operators, course help, approved data destinations, independent restores and usable exports. Resource approval does not fund these duties.

Decide from evidence

Twelve pre-class checks. No full-course dependency until capacity, software, access, support and recovery are demonstrated.
