Release funding in stages

Propose a first-stage ceiling of $8,000 for component tests, GPU benchmarks, allocation preparation, institutional review and the ten-volunteer canary, within the $35,000 total envelope. This is an authorization cap, not an additional charge or a priced provider test. Release up to the remaining $27,000 only after the readiness evidence is reviewed; cancel/revise if the canary cannot support the core workflows.

Faculty, Libraries and finance effort is modeled as in-kind. If no written workload commitment is available, switch the corresponding row to Cash and recost; do not assume the labor is available at no cost.

A campus storage comparison, with its limits

For eligible open research data only: three TB of campus rental at $47.35/TB/year is $142.05/year, charged for the fiscal-year maximum without proration. A separate 2.6 TB active Tier 2 copy would have a modeled 1.6 TB paid overage × $23 × six months = $220.80; combined example $362.85. Actual classes, billing and data suitability need confirmation.

The 2.6 TB backup comparison comes from the larger Jetstream2 route-assessment scenario, not the initial 440 GB student/shared data planning baseline. Conditional public/open-research-data comparison only. Campus rental is charged for the fiscal-year maximum and is not prorated; it is not backed up. Tier 2 estimate assumes 1.6 TB above the free first TB stays active for six months; actual storage-class/billing behavior varies. Glacier/Deep Glacier restoration can delay access. Confirm classroom eligibility, approved data classification, billing account, rates and connectivity. Retain a warm independent copy; neither these services nor R-DAS is approved here for private student work or grade records. The $2,000 reserve is used in the main budget instead of treating this comparison as a complete backup quote.

What is outside this estimate

Excludes normal course instruction/grading, new staff recruitment, major platform development, laptops, paid software, optional GPU/model charges and institutional indirect costs beyond the assumed loaded rates. Additional scope requires a new estimate. No student-paid account or personal payment card is proposed.
