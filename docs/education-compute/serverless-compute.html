Cloud credits & GPUs¶

Supplemental reference · Browse supplements · Current report

Modal is a conditional instructor experiment, not the classroom baseline. Its documented $30/workspace/month credit requires a payment method and the Starter workspace has three seats. CPU, GPU and memory share the allowance. No card, credit redemption, grant or workload has been tested here.

With $0 cash, do not depend on paid conversion. Use Kaggle/free Colab for preliminary GPU benchmarks, then replay on awarded ACCESS CPU + GPU resources. Confirm eligible grant funding before any paid use.

Provider documentation reviewed October 16, 2026. Pricing and credits can change; follow the dated sources.

9 resources

Conditional recurring credit

Modal

DS: a small pinned training/inference run. MLIS: public-corpus OCR, embeddings or retrieval experiments.

Access: $30/workspace/month; three Starter seats

Limits and sources

Payment method required; GPU, CPU and memory share credits. Set explicit usage and net-spend limits. Shared Endpoints tokens cost extra. Academic grant is an application, not funding in hand.

DS/MLIS component hosting if funded; open-source runtime may warrant later engineering assessment.

Access: $20/month Developer includes $20 credits; one seat

Limits and sources

June free-$30 comparison is stale against current direct plan table. Prepaid deployments need credits; warm/idle settings can bill. BYOC is not free compute.

Paid usage / possible trial

Cerebrium

DS model serving; MLIS public-corpus embeddings or transcription.

Access: Free Hobby subscription; no recurring compute credit verified

Limits and sources

Free plan fee and included storage do not make execution free. Trials are discretionary/time-limited and may transition to paid billing.

Bounded free machines

Paperspace Gradient

Small DS training and public/synthetic MLIS corpus notebooks.

Access: Free CPU / M4000 GPU subject to availability; 5 GB

Limits and sources

Six-hour free-session maximum, one-hour idle stop, one free notebook at once; free notebooks are public. Check old GPU/runtime fit and actual signup/payment requirements.

Promotional / account-dependent

Lightning AI

DS/MLIS notebook preparation and small tests within actual credit balance.

Access: One free CPU Studio; GPU credit amount and expiry vary

Limits and sources

Four-hour CPU restart boundary; first 10 GB storage free. Do not promise a fixed monthly GPU grant or count paid academic discounts as free compute.
