Describe a computing resource need¶

For College of Information Science faculty and students: describe the research or teaching activity and what is getting in the way. You do not need to know GPU hours or a budget. Include software, data, people, devices or hosting needs; AI is optional. Unknown quantities can stay unknown.

Working-group point people: Barney Maccabe and Greg Chism. Preparing a brief does not send it to them.

The needs brief and source-backed template work locally. Nothing is submitted to the working group. Optional Draft with Luna sends a reviewed public/synthetic brief for AI processing only when you choose it; the service does not save the brief or draft. Use non-identifying descriptions; keep private research, student records and confidential staffing information out of this public planning tool.

    Develop a resource request

    This local template turns your brief into a reviewable request and adds documented resource routes. It does not use an LLM or decide eligibility. Candidate routes need owner confirmation.

    Optional Luna draft

    Use GPT-6 Luna to rewrite your brief and the dated resource facts into a short first draft. Review the result yourself. The local template above is always available.

    Limited pilot: five attempts per network each day. Your local template remains available.

    Usage limits and data handling

    Shared allowance: 20 attempts/day, 200/month, up to 1,000 output tokens each. Failed attempts count; there are no automatic retries. The application keeps allowance counters and rotating network hashes, not your brief or generated text. OpenAI processes the requested text under its API terms; store:false is requested, not a zero-retention guarantee.

    Campus alternative: copy or download the public/synthetic prompt and paste it into Arizona AI Assistant ↗.
