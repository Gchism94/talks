Proposed acceptance register · no checks run

Before this becomes required coursework.

Twelve checks, with an accountable owner and concrete evidence. The current status is 0 of 12 confirmed. An allocation alone does not change that status.

Targets such as five-minute p95 login, 24-hour RPO and one-business-day recovery/response are proposed acceptance criteria to negotiate and measure. They are not CloudBank guarantees or achieved service levels.

Operations · Before request

Planned — not run

1 · Scope, owners and funds

Owner: Sponsor + service owner

Assign eligible PI, two course leads, primary and distinct backup operator; approve staged funding and term coverage.

Evidence required: Written roster of owners, budget/workload commitments and escalation cover.

Stop if: No named operator, backup or approved operating period.

Access · Before request / gate to class

Planned — not run

2 · Provider fit and award

Owner: Service owner + CloudBank

Provider confirms the general ACCESS Classroom route, requested 80 student + six staff identities, resource limits, software, course dates and term/export rules. Clarify one versus two projects.

Evidence required: Provider response and approved award/resource configuration, recorded privately.

Stop if: Assuming unspecified limits, general broker terms or automatic renewal.

Access · Canary; repeat before week 1

Planned — not run

3 · Login, isolation and removal

Owner: Operator + course leads

Every approved student/staff test account authenticates; student A cannot read/write student B files or instructor grading areas; removal/revocation works.

Evidence required: Access matrix and dated test results; no passwords or roster identifiers in public reports.

Stop if: Access leakage, shared credentials or unverified removal.

Teaching · Canary

Planned — not run

4 · Complete CPU assignment

Owner: Each course lead

Both complete representative assignments run on the approved CPU image from fresh workspaces; no paid account or GPU is required. Pin the approved environment and input checksums.

Evidence required: Clean-run outputs, environment manifest, resource measurements and a known-good baseline.

Stop if: Missing packages, memory failure, unapproved app or required GPU dependency.

Access · 2–4 weeks before term

Planned — not run

5 · Class concurrency

Owner: Operator + provider

With provider permission, rehearse 80 simultaneous students plus agreed staff use in three representative sessions. Proposed p95 login-to-workspace ≤5 minutes; no unresolved assignment-blocking launch failure.

Evidence required: Dated latency distribution, workspace success count, quota/usage forecast and provider-agreed concurrency.

Stop if: No permission/capacity evidence; peak spec exceeds awarded limits.

Teaching · Canary; repeat after image update

Planned — not run

6 · Save, restart and export

Owner: Course leads + TAs

Edit, save, stop/restart and recover a notebook/data package; verify filesystem location and retention. Export and re-import it into the CPU fallback.

Evidence required: Saved-file checksums, restart/export receipt and fresh-environment reopen.

Stop if: Student work depends on an unsaved session or cannot be exported.

Teaching · Before graded use

Planned — not run

7 · Submission and grade receipts

Owner: Course leads

Distribute a versioned assignment, collect it, reconcile all test submissions, preserve student authorship and instructor judgment, and import/export a test grade receipt through the approved LMS workflow.

Evidence required: Submission manifest and grade reconciliation results in the approved institutional destination.

Stop if: A leaderboard/auto-score substitutes for the authoritative grade, or a missing receipt goes undetected.

Operations · Before student data

Planned — not run

8 · Data destinations and rights

Owner: IT/privacy reviewer + Libraries

Approve provider and backup data destinations, lawful/appropriate datasets, access roles and retention. Keep grades/rosters in the approved LMS/institutional systems; publish only aggregate results.

Teaching · Before course admission

Planned — not run

9 · Accessible essential workflows

Owner: Instructional designer + course leads

Rehearse keyboard and screen-reader access, readable instructions and equivalent small-data CPU/offline activity; resolve barriers in essential workflows.

Evidence required: User/task review and course-lead sign-off, with accommodations and fallback instructions.

Recovery · Before course admission

Planned — not run

10 · Independent restore

Owner: Primary + backup operators

Make independent copies in approved destinations and restore onto a different host/environment. Proposed RPO ≤24 hours and RTO ≤one business day; measure both.

Evidence required: Restore logs, independent file checksums, recovery timing and backup-operator runbook rehearsal.

Stop if: No independent copy or unmeasured recovery; relying only on snapshots or cold archival retrieval.

Operations · Before week 1; weekly

Planned — not run

11 · Support and fallback

Owner: Owner + TAs + operators

Publish actual staffed help windows and escalation route; rehearse an outage with CPU/small-data fallback. Proposed first response within one staffed business day; do not promise 24/7 support.

Evidence required: Coverage schedule, ticket routing, outage exercise and fallback delivery proof.

Stop if: Unfunded coverage, unreachable operator or missed class deadline without substitution.

Recovery · Before start; execute near term end

Planned — not run

12 · Expiry and teach-out

Owner: Service owner + course leads + Libraries

Schedule alerts 60/30/14/7 days before expiry; decide continuation at least 30 days before. Rehearse learner/grade export, retention and deletion; verify closure on the approved dates.

Evidence required: End-of-term runbook, sample exports, provider confirmation of exit window and deletion/renewal decision.

Stop if: Relying on grace periods, unconfirmed education extension or archival deposits of private coursework.

Who admits the course

Each course lead signs off the assignment, accessibility and grading path; the operator and backup sign off access/capacity and recovery; IT/privacy approves data destinations; the service owner and college sponsor verify funding, coverage and remaining risks. Record names, dates and private supporting artifacts in the institutional register, not this public website. Classroom admission requires all applicable checks, or a reviewed substitute that demonstrably meets the same need.

A failed core check keeps the full course out of the pilot. A failed optional component stays out while the CPU baseline proceeds if its checks passed. Recheck affected gates after image, policy, staffing or provider changes.

Proposal, not a service announcement. No CloudBank request, allocation, staffing appointment or pilot budget is approved by this site. Provider policies and proposed tests are separate from completed classroom evidence.
