## Resources we can build on

Start with college and campus compute, software, data services and technical expertise. The [resource inventory](inventory.html) brings access, costs, contacts and limitations together. Match a service to the activity and confirm capacity with its owner.

## The growing gap

<div class="guide-thesis">What we have previously relied on can no longer be guaranteed</div>

Demand spans compute, software, data and the people who make them usable. Meanwhile, some free services have closed, shortened access or tightened limits. Useful options remain, but a previous semester’s setup is no longer a sufficient continuity plan. The [report](report.html#the-growing-gap) distinguishes documented changes from unknowns; it does not measure a college-wide shortfall.

## The opportunity

<div class="guide-route"><div><span>01 / CONNECT</span><strong>Make existing resources usable</strong><p>Clarify access, onboarding and support before duplicating services.</p></div><div><span>02 / SCOPE</span><strong>Describe the work and the gap</strong><p>Capture the activity, barriers and support needs. Technical quantities can remain unknown.</p></div><div><span>03 / TEST</span><strong>Build evidence for shared routes</strong><p>Compare reusable environments, shared expertise, partnerships and allocation-backed services.</p></div></div>

For the instructional pilot, a measured **ACCESS CPU + GPU allocation** is the proposed route; eligible **NAIRR Classroom** pilots may complement it. Awards, operations and classroom access still need confirmation. The group can compare this with other shared-service approaches.
